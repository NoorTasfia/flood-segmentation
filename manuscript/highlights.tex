\begin{center}\Large\textbf{Highlights}\end{center}

\begin{itemize}
\item CPU and browser benchmark of three lightweight flood-segmentation UNets.
\item On saturated FSSD, pretrained students match the teacher without distillation.
\item INT8 quantization is architecture-dependent: Lite0 holds, two backbones collapse.
\item FSSD models false-alarm on 70--100\% of dry scenes; retraining cuts this to 3--9\%.
\item On harder data, distillation lifts flood IoU but copies teacher false alarms.
\end{itemize}
