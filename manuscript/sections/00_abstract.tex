\begin{abstract}
Flood-segmentation models are usually judged by accuracy alone, yet responders run them on laptops, tablets and browsers, where latency, quantization behaviour and false alarms decide whether a model can be used. \emph{FloodLite} benchmarks three lightweight UNet students (MobileNetV3-Small, EfficientNet-Lite0, MobileViT-XXS) and a UNet$+$EfficientNet-B0 teacher under three-fold cross-validation on the Flood Semantic Segmentation Dataset (FSSD). We measure FP32 and INT8 latency on an Apple M2~Pro CPU (ONNX Runtime) and in browser WebAssembly, and ablate response, feature and combined knowledge distillation (KD). On this saturated benchmark the ImageNet-pretrained students match or exceed the teacher without distillation (best: MobileViT-XXS, IoU $\bestStudentIoU \pm \bestStudentStd$ vs.\ $\teacherIoU \pm \teacherStd$), and no KD configuration helps. INT8 post-training quantization is architecture-dependent: EfficientNet-Lite0 loses 0.043 IoU, while MobileNetV3-Small and MobileViT-XXS collapse by 0.45--0.46 under the same recipe. EfficientNet-Lite0 INT8 runs at \deployableMTwoFPS~FPS on the laptop CPU, $2.2\times$ faster than the teacher on the same runtime and $3.83\times$ smaller. Zero-shot tests on overlap-verified external data expose a failure that FSSD metrics hide. Every FSSD-trained model collapses on Sentinel-2 imagery (IoU $\approx 0.16$), and, because FSSD contains no water-free images, every one predicts water on 72--100\% of dry scenes. Retraining on a combined corpus with 855 water-free images cuts student false-alarm rates to 3--9\% and raises flood-only IoU on FloodNet from about 0.5 to 0.70--0.78. On this less saturated corpus distillation does raise flood-only IoU ($+0.03$ to $+0.07$, significant for all three students), but it also passes on the teacher's false alarms: distilled students fire on the teacher's false-alarm scenes more than twice as often as undistilled ones. A teacher should therefore be checked for the failure that matters in deployment before it is used for distillation. Code, checkpoints and ONNX exports are released.
\end{abstract}
